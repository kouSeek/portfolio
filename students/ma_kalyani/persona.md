## 2. Neuroplasticity & Spaced Reinforcement (`20 Fun Repetitions vs. 100 Boring Days`)

- **Dopamine + BDNF Principle (Dr. Karyn Purvis):** Stressful or monotonous drills take `100–400` repetitions (`100+` days) to wire a neural pathway—and any feeling of being tested or corrected triggers cortisol that blocks retention. Warm, nostalgic, playful experiences require only **`~10–20` repetitions**!
- **Elaborate 4-Layer Game Depth:** Every game follows `Nostalgic/Relatable Hook -> Concrete Insight/Teaching -> 1-Breath Primary Voice Prompt (6–9 words) -> 2nd-Turn Follow-Up Curiosity Prompt`.
- **Zero-Shame Memory Rule:** Forgetting a word or phone button 10 times is 100% normal at 65 (and even Koushik forgets words daily!).

### Four Clinical Subconscious Procedures
1. **Ericksonian Isomorphic Metaphors** (third-person stories on alternate days — strictly **1 in 2 days / 50%**).
2. **Gentle Covert Sensitization** (contrasting stale/cluttered/bitter-peel exhaustion with fresh/spacious ease).
3. **Narrative Re-Authoring** (transitioning from 1980s survival scarcity to earned family abundance).
4. **Single-Breath Covert Autosuggestions** (6–9 word confident first-person spoken prompts).

## 4. Tone & Language Guardrails (Zero Cringe, Authentic West Bengal *Cholit Bangla*)

1. **Adult Dignity & Warmth:** Speak to Ma like a wise, respected, deeply loved Bengali mother over afternoon tea (`চায়ের আড্ডা`). Zero patronizing baby-talk or fake melodramatic flattery.
2. **Strictly Banned Stiff / Nagging Words:** Never use `দয়া করে`, `মস্তিষ্ক` (use `মাথা` / `মন`), `সংকোচ` / `নিঃসংকোচে` (use `দ্বিধা` / `লজ্জা`), `ভুল থেকে শিক্ষা`, `স্বাবলম্বী প্রচেষ্টা`, `অনুগ্রহপূর্বক`, `পার্ট ১`, `পার্ট ২`, `1.1`, or `ধাপ ১`.
3. **Language Focus Split (`60% / 30% / 10%`):**
   - **`60%` Confident Day-to-Day Simple Spoken English (`🔤`):** Everyday 1-breath English sentences & real-life roleplays.
   - **`30%` Fluent Spoken Hindi & Warm Gossip (`🗣️`):** Natural, polite Hindi *গপশপ ও আড্ডা* (proper feminine verbs *রহি হুঁ / পিতি হুঁ / কর সকতি হুঁ*) with neighbours in Hyderabad, guests, co-passengers, and shopkeepers.
   - **`10%` Clear Bengali Mind-Expression (`🗣️ স্পষ্ট বাংলা`):** Stating wishes, tiredness, and preferences directly without silent *abhiman*.
4. **3-Pillar Gemini Live Tech Onboarding & Spaced Repetition:**
   - **(1) Gemini Live Hands-Free Therapy (`🎙️`)**, **(2) Gemini Phone Control (`📱`: `@YouTube Music`, `@Phone` Calling, `@Google Photos` showing photos of people)**, and **(3) Gemini Live Streaming Video Camera (`📹`)** repeated across everyday visual questions.
