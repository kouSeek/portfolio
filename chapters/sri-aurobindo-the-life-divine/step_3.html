Step 3 · Involution and Evolution — Consciousness Sleeping in Matter

"What emerges in evolution was already involved in the beginning. Matter could not evolve life if life were not secretly sleeping in the stone."

— Sri Aurobindo

The Law of Involution

How can a non-living mineral soup suddenly produce self-replicating DNA, eyes that see, and minds that write symphonies and calculate tensor calculus? Materialistic science claims order arose from random collisions over time.

Aurobindo reveals the deeper metaphysical law: **Evolution is simply the reverse journey of Involution.**

Before the Big Bang, the infinite, unconditioned Consciousness (*Sachchidananda*) voluntarily descended, stepping down its frequency through subtle planes of consciousness:

- Supermind -> Overmind -> Intuitive Mind -> Illumined Mind -> Higher Mind -> Vital (Life) -> Matter.

At the lowest point of this cosmic descent, consciousness compacted itself into the dense, unconscious atom.

Because divine consciousness was **involved** (hidden) inside matter, matter was pregnant with life. When conditions were ready, life sprang forth. Because life was pregnant with mind, mind sprang forth. And because mind is pregnant with the Supermind, the Supermind must inevitably manifest on earth.
