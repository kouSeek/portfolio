The Electrical Model

1. **Ida (Sensory / Left Side):** Cooling, reflective, receptive.

2. **Pingala (Motor / Right Side):** Heating, expressive, active.

3. **The Short Circuit:** In the untrained person, nerve currents alternate frantically between left and right, burning out autonomic circuits and causing mental exhaustion.

4. **Sushumna (The Open Channel):** When the two currents are harmonized through balanced breathing, nerve force enters the central canal of the spinal cord, unleashing limitless focus and inner strength.
