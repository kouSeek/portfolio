Ch 1 · Chapter I · Causes of Chitta, Mitahara & Shakti-Chalana
The two primary causes of the unquiet mind (Chitta): Vasanas (latent mental impressions) and Prana (the breath). Controlling Prana through disciplined diet (Mitahara), Padmasana or Vajrasana, and the rhythmic agitation of Kundalini (Shakti-Chalana).
