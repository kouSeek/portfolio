Step 1 · The Chariot Architecture: Reins, Horses & Road (Katha)

Yama teaches Nachiketa the timeless mechanics of cognitive control:

*Know the Atman as the passenger seated within the chariot, and the physical body as the chariot itself. Know the intellect (Buddhi) as the charioteer, and the mind (Manas) as the reins. The senses (Indriyas) are the galloping horses, and the objects of sensory perception are the paths they traverse.* (Katha 1.3.3-4)

The First-Principles Breakdown

1. **The Sleeping Driver:** When you open a phone or browse aimlessly, your Buddhi (deliberate executive function) is unconscious. The sensory horses (algorithm feeds, bright alerts) bolt in 15 different directions.

2. **The Tension in the Reins:** You cannot control runaway horses by yelling at them. You steady them through the firm, calm tension on the reins (anchoring attention in a single concrete physical action).

3. **The Sovereign Passenger:** The core observing awareness (Atman) is never harmed by the terrain or the bumps in the road. It sits quietly, observing without distress.
