Step 3 · The 3 Bandhas & Nadanusandhana (Awakening Inner Sound)

Hydraulic Locking Mechanisms

1. **Mula Bandha (Perineal Lock):**

- Press the left heel against the perineum; contract the pelvic floor and anal sphincter upward.

- *Physical Mechanism:* Arrests the downward evacuation of Apana Vayu, forcing it to reverse direction and rise toward the navel.

2. **Jalandhara Bandha (Cervical Lock):**

- Contract the throat and press the chin firmly against the jugular notch between the clavicles.

- *Physical Mechanism:* Compresses the carotid sinuses, lowering systemic blood pressure and preventing cerebral pressure surges during Kumbhaka.

3. **Uddiyana Bandha (Abdominal Vacuum):**

- After complete exhalation, pull the navel and abdomen deeply inward and upward toward the thoracic cavity.

- *Physical Mechanism:* Negative intra-thoracic pressure draws the unified Prana-Apana directly into the central spinal channel (Sushumna Nadi).

The Four Sound Stages (Nadanusandhana)

- **Arambha Avastha:** The heart knot (Brahma Granthi) is pierced; tinkling sounds like tiny ornaments, bells, or ankle chimes emerge in the right ear.

- **Ghata Avastha:** The throat knot (Vishnu Granthi) is pierced; kettle-drums, mridangas, and resonant gongs sound within the cranium.

- **Parichaya Avastha:** The third knot (Rudra Granthi) is approached; sounds like massive brass cymbals and thunder fill the consciousness.

- **Nishpatti Avastha:** Complete dissolution; pure flutes, lutes, and humming bees sound as the mind merges into Nirvikalpa Samadhi.
