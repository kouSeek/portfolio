Step 1 · Clearing the 72,000 Nadis & Shatkarmas (Start Here)

"Asanas make a person firm, free from maladies, and light of limb. When the nadis are purified, the prana easily pierces the mouth of the Sushumna."

— Swami Swatmarama, *Hatha Yoga Pradipika* 1.17, 2.41

The Physical Plumbing of Consciousness

Swami Swatmarama opens the *Hatha Yoga Pradipika* with an uncompromising mechanical truth: **You cannot force a restless, agitated mind into meditation while the physical nervous system is clogged with metabolic waste.**

Traditional Raja Yoga tells you to sit still and quiet your thoughts. But if your autonomic nervous system is stressed, your diaphragm is rigid, and your lymphatic pathways are congested, sitting still only creates agony and mental restlessness. Swatmarama created Hatha Yoga as the bodily staircase (*sopana*) to Raja Yoga: you alter mental consciousness by first altering physical mechanics.

The body contains 72,000 subtle neural and energetic conduits (*Nadis*). When these channels are filled with metabolic toxins (*Mala*), vital energy (*Prana*) cannot circulate freely. Swatmarama prescribes two foundation pillars:

1. **Asanas (Postures):** Selected postures (such as Siddhasana, Padmasana, and Paschimottanasana) are designed to massage the abdominal viscera, tone the spinal column, and establish physical stillness (*Sthiram*).

2. **Shatkarmas (The Six Purifications):** For individuals with excess mucus (Kapha) or fat, he prescribes physical cleansing:

- **Neti:** Nasal cleansing with saline water to decongest the cranial pathways and sinus cavities.

- **Dhauti:** Cleansing the esophagus and stomach.

- **Nauli:** Isolating and churning the rectus abdominis muscles to stimulate mesenteric blood flow and ignite digestive fire.

- **Basti:** Colon irrigation.

- **Kapalabhati:** Rapid diaphragmatic exhales to oxygenate the brain and purge stagnant carbon dioxide from the lower lungs.

- **Trataka:** Unblinking gazing at a steady flame to focus attention and stimulate the optic nerve and pineal gland.

Once the physical vessel is cleaned, breath regulation can begin without triggering headaches or nervous exhaustion.
