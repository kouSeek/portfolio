# 🌸 কল্যাণী (Kalyani) — Master 21-Day Curriculum Blueprint (`curriculum.md`)

This Markdown document is the **single source of truth for what Ma learns, practices, and experiences** across the 21-day `🌸 কল্যাণী` companion web app (`Days 1–21` Foundation) and her local **Client-Side Android Gemini App**.

---

## 1. Learner Persona & Core Goal

- **Learner:** Shikha Chakraborty ("Ma" / `মা`, age ~65, Dum Dum Cantonment, Kolkata; childhood roots in Ranaghat, Nadia). Shares inattentive ADHD, a soft nature, and deep empathy with Koushik, **but has very low analytical IQ and low ambition** (whereas Koushik has high IQ and ambition)—so every single game must stay ultra-simple, concrete, sensory, and completely free of academic or ambitious pressure.
- **Food Freedom & Dietary Truth:** Ma **hates eggs, soybean, and mushroom**, and **does not prefer eating dal or paneer**, yet hides this out of fear that people will judge or insult her. Never suggest those foods for her; empower her to voice her true food tastes openly and unapologetically.
- **Family Ecosystem:** Deeply loved by Koushik (Senior AI Engineer at Google, Hyderabad) and her daughter Kankana ("Didi", with Pratik, Ayush, and Aishee). Unlike Ma and Koushik, **Didi has zero ADHD—she is a strong, independent, sharp, materialistic woman who naturally loves everybody and keeps warm rapport, but heavy marital fights, family pressures, and depression have made her emotionally numb, rude, and self-centered** as a survival shell. Help Ma understand Didi's numbness with compassion so Ma never internalizes Didi's rudeness.
- **Physical Boundary:** **Zero physiotherapy or physical exercise prompts** inside Kalyani or Gemini (handled separately by her live in-home personal trainer).
- **Daily Structure:** Every day (`Days 1–21`) presents **6 deep, zero-rush games** (`4–6` range), each with a concrete teaching target and a 2-turn spoken prompt arc (`prompt_bn` + `followup_prompt_bn`) spoken in warm West Bengal colloquial Bengali (`চলিত বাংলা`).

## 2. Spoken Language Focus Split (`60% English : 30% Hindi : 10% Bengali`)

Across the 21-day curriculum (`2` dedicated communication/language games every day = `42` language games total) and inside Client-Side Gemini's ongoing coaching, language development follows an exact **60 / 30 / 10** focus distribution:

1. **`60%` Focus — Confident Day-to-Day Simple Spoken English (`🔤` — ~25 Games across 21 Days):**
   - **Goal:** Speak simple, everyday English words and short 6–9 word single-breath sentences aloud with ease, natural flow, and zero hesitation.
   - **Core Vocabulary & Real-Life Usage:** `Comfortable, Peaceful, Actually, Prefer, Choice, Definitely, Relax, Manage`, plus everyday conversational English (`Thank you, Please, Take your time, Excuse me, Wonderful, Enjoy, Available, Fresh, Convenient, Easy, Appointment, Schedule, Appreciate, Helpful, Check, Confirm, Speaker, Play, Pause, Next, Total, Date, Bill`) for shops, doctors, airports, cafes, and home life.

2. **`30%` Focus — Speaking Fluently & Warmly Gossiping/Chatting in Hindi (`🗣️` — ~13 Games across 21 Days):**
   - **Goal:** Speak clean, natural, grammatical everyday Hindi (with proper feminine verb agreement such as *রহি হুঁ, পিতি হুঁ, যাউঙ্গি, কর সকতি হুঁ*) so Ma can chat effortlessly, bond, and enjoy warm *adda*/gossip (`গল্পগুজব`) with neighbours in Hyderabad, society friends, guests, co-passengers on flights/trains, tailors, and shopkeepers—without shyness or awkward *Bangla-mixed* Hindi.
   - **Topics:** Friendly neighbour gossip & greetings (*“আরে বহনজি, আজকল ক্যায়া চল রহা হ্যায়?”*), discussing weather/food/sarees/festivals/serials, Hyderabad daily life, travel, cabs, markets, and hospitality—always written in easy Bengali phonetic script.

3. **`10%` Focus — Expressing Her Mind & Clear Communication in Bengali (`🗣️ বাংলায় স্পষ্ট মনের কথা` — ~4 Games across 21 Days, Every 5th Day):**
   - **Goal:** Articulate her own wishes, boundaries, physical tiredness, food preferences, and emotions **clearly, calmly, and directly in Bengali** without holding silent hurt (*অভিমান*), hinting indirectly, or feeling guilty for stating what she needs.

## 3. Technical Onboarding & Gemini Live Mastery (3 Core Tech Pillars Through Spaced Repetition)

Technology learning centers on **3 transformative capabilities of Android Gemini Live**, introduced via a clear **Quick Onboarding Sequence in Days 1–3** and reinforced continuously through **spaced repetition across Days 4–21** until effortless:

1. **Tech Pillar A — Talking to Gemini Live as a Personal Therapy & Heart-to-Heart Session (`🎙️ Gemini Live Voice`):**
   - Tapping the **Gemini Live** waveform button (`✨🎙️`) to put the phone down on a pillow or table and have an uninterrupted, hands-free, natural spoken Bengali conversation—pausing, thinking, sighing, or interrupting freely without pressing any mic button repeatedly.

2. **Tech Pillar B — Using Gemini Live to Operate & Control Her Phone (`📱 Hands-Free Phone Control`):**
   - **Quick Onboarding + Repetition** showing Ma what Gemini can directly control on her phone by voice:
     - **YouTube Music (`@YouTube Music`):** Playing any Bengali/Hindi song by name or a single remembered lyric line.
     - **Calling Family & Contacts (`@Phone`):** Calling Koushik, Didi, or family hands-free on speakerphone (*“জেমিনি, কৌশিককে স্পিকারে ফোন লাগিয়ে দাও”*).
     - **Showing Photos of People & Memories (`@Google Photos`):** Opening photos of loved ones and trips directly from Google Photos (*“জেমিনি, গুগল ফটোস থেকে কৌশিকের ছবি দেখাও”*, *“আয়ুষ আর ঐশীর ছবি দেখাও”*, *“হায়দরাবাদ বেড়ানোর ছবিগুলো খুলে দাও”*).
     - **Phone Utilities & Safety:** Setting medicine/tea alarms & reminders, checking weather before walks, using `Back` (`◀️`) / `Home` (`⚪`) fearlessly, and hanging up after a 5-second breath on unknown OTP/bank scam calls.

3. **Tech Pillar C — Using Gemini Live Streaming Video Camera (`📹 Live Camera Vision`) for Any Day-to-Day Question:**
   - **High-Frequency Spaced Repetition** teaching Ma to tap the **Live Video Camera (`📹`)** inside Gemini Live and point her phone at anything around her in real time to ask random, practical everyday questions:
     - Reading medicine strips, dosages & expiry dates (`EXP`)
     - Understanding AC remote (`25–26°C`), TV remote, or washing machine buttons
     - Showing fridge vegetables/ingredients to ask what tasty Bengali dish to cook
     - Reading electricity bills, courier labels, bank letters, or prescriptions in Bengali
     - Comparing two sarees or blouse colors from her almirah
     - Checking why a balcony hibiscus/tulsi leaf is turning yellow
     - Showing her notebook drawings to get warm artistic encouragement and color ideas

## 4. Curiosity, Culture & Dual-Wing Psychological Care

1. **Song Lyrics that Spark Curiosity & Meaning (`🎵` — Daily via `@YouTube Music`):**
   - Listening to poetic lyrics from Rabindra Sangeet, Hemanta, Sandhya, Manna Dey, Lata, Kishore, Shyamal Mitra, Pratima, Geeta Dutt, Manabendra, D.L. Roy, and Bhupen Hazarika, then unpacking the deeper philosophy and backstage story of the lyrics.

2. **Innocent Childlike Comedy, Sweet Gods (Zero Fights), Witty Kings & Cute Animals (`🌍` — Daily):**
   - Instead of dry technical science or war epics (Ma dislikes fights, so **never** use Ramayana or Mahabharata), every day brings effortless smiles and wonder through:
     - **Innocent Comedy & Legendary Kings:** Nadia's Maharaja Krishnachandra & Gopal Bhar (`মহারাজ কৃষ্ণচন্দ্র ও গোপাল ভাঁড়`), Birbal, Tenali Raman, and harmless household humour.
     - **Sweet, Peaceful Stories of Gods:** Baby Gopal (`ননীচোরা বালগোপালের মাখন চুরি`), Shiva-Parvati & little Ganesha circling his parents (`বাবা-মা-ই তো বিশ্বব্রহ্মাণ্ড`), Puri's Lord Jagannath & Maa Lakshmi's rasagolla *manbhanjan*, Maa Annapurna in Kashi, and Maa Sarada's pet parrot *Gangaram* & Sri Ramakrishna's childlike love for jalebis.
     - **Cute Animals & Inspiring Lives:** Baby elephants, sea otters holding paws while sleeping, forgetful squirrels planting forests, penguins gifting smooth pebbles, cows & calves' best friendships, and relatable inspiring figures (Maa Sarada, Rani Rashmoni, Subhasini Mistry, J.C. Bose, Nandalal Bose, Sister Nivedita, Sudha Murty, Satyajit Ray).

3. **Covert CBT Therapy for Old Negative Habits (`📖` — Strictly 1 in 2 Days / Even Days Only = `50%`):**
   - Third-person Ericksonian stories dissolving old scarcity/anxiety habits: (a) First-press lemon juice vs. bitter peel (*লেবুর তিতো খোসা* — dropping exhausting toil for 2 rupees), (b) eating fresh fruit & wearing good sarees today, (c) guilt-free AC/fan/light comfort, (d) using 2–3 notebook pages generously without fear of wasting paper/paint, (e) decluttering old plastic bags & broken containers, and (f) dropping catastrophic worry.

4. **Personal Conversation as Psychotherapy (`💬` — Odd Days = `50%` + Ongoing in Client Gemini):**
   - Extraordinary psychotherapist-guided **Reminiscence Therapy** (reviving golden memories of Ranaghat childhood, school, festivals, Baba, raising Koushik & Didi) paired with **Safe Cathartic Trauma Release** (gently surfacing and releasing post-2016 grief, feeling misunderstood, silent *abhiman*, and caretaker fatigue so her mind feels crystal-clear and light).
